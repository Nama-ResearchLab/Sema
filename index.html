Empirical Methodology & Experimental Protocol

Timing was captured using monotonic hardware timestamp counters (TSC) via rdtsc on x86_64 and cntvct_el0 on ARM64. Pinned to an isolated physical core to prevent thread migration. Standard deviation was computed with Bessel's correction: σ = √[ (1/(N-1)) Σ(x_i - μ)² ]. Outlier samples exceeding 3× interquartile range (IQR) were analyzed for OS scheduler interrupts and reported in P99.9 columns.
